## Broker Import

![Broker Import](/images/changelog/2026-06/broker-import.jpg)

A much easier way to keep Lauolon in sync.

- We added the first version of Broker Import.
- You can now import your portfolio and buy/sell transaction history from supported broker exports.
- Lauolon can create missing positions, import transaction records, skip duplicates, and recalculate your portfolio history automatically.
- The first supported broker is Trade Republic.

This means you can export your data from your broker, import it into Lauolon, and repeat the process whenever you want to keep your portfolio updated.

If your broker is not supported yet, please get in touch at [support@lauolon.com](mailto:support@lauolon.com).

## Custom Categories

![Custom Categories](/images/changelog/2026-06/custom-categories.jpg)

Organize your portfolio your way.

- You can now create your own custom categories.
- Assets can be labeled using the categories that make sense to you.
- This gives you more flexibility than the default Lauolon category system.

## 🇬🇧 London and 🇰🇼 Kuwait Market Support

- Lauolon now supports London-listed securities.
- GBp / GBX-denominated securities are now supported.
- Lauolon now supports Kuwait-listed securities, including securities quoted in KWF / Kuwaiti fils.
